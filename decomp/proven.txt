# the shipped library BIT-FOR-BIT over 900 compounding steps. The solver's

# --- kea objects that are NOT evidenced, and exactly why ---------------------
#
# These all print `trajectory bit-identical` and none of that is evidence.
#
# keaCalcAcceleration_vanilla   RAN 900 times, and the probe cannot see it. Its
#                     arithmetic is accel[i] = invmass * force[i] plus a torque
